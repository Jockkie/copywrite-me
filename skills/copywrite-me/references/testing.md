# Testing copy

A scorecard predicts; a test proves. Every delivery ends with one test suggestion.

## What to test first
Test the element with the biggest effect on the metric: headline or hook, then the offer framing, then the call to action, then proof placement. Do not test button colours before the promise is right.

## Simple tests without tools
- **Email:** send subject line A to half the list and B to the other half; compare opens and, more importantly, clicks or replies.
- **Social:** post variant A and, a week later at the same time, variant B; compare saves, shares, comments and profile visits. Note that reach varies a lot per post; repeat before drawing conclusions.
- **Landing page:** run A for two weeks, then B for two weeks with similar traffic; compare conversion rate.
- **Ads:** most ad platforms split-test natively; follow their current guidance.
- **Sales conversations:** use opening line A with five prospects and B with the next five.

## Reading results
- Small numbers lie. Under roughly 100 conversions per variant, treat differences as hints.
- Change one thing at a time.
- Record in the brief: date, variants, metric, result, decision.

## Feed back
A winning phrase goes into `business-context.md` under positioning or customer phrases, so the next text starts from it.

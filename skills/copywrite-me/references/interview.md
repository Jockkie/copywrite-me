# Interview: eight topics and Gate 1

One question at a time. If a Market This dossier or brand brief exists, confirm instead of asking again. For short formats (a post, an email) the interview can take five minutes; for a sales page take the time.

| # | Topic | Core questions | Done when |
|---|-------|----------------|-----------|
| 1 | Goal and action | What should the reader do after reading? What counts as success (clicks, replies, sales, sign-ups)? | One action and one success metric. |
| 2 | Reader | Who exactly reads this? What is going on in their life or work right now? What do they want, fear, believe? | One specific reader, with a situation and a desire. |
| 3 | Awareness | Do they know they have the problem? Do they know solutions exist? Do they know you? | Awareness level chosen (see `frameworks.md`). |
| 4 | Offer | What do they get, for what price, how fast, with what risk? What makes it different? | Offer in one sentence, price and the main difference. |
| 5 | Proof | Results, numbers, testimonials (with permission), cases, credentials, media. | Proof listed and labeled real; or "none yet" stated. |
| 6 | Objections | Why do people hesitate or say no? What do they ask before buying? | Top three objections. |
| 7 | Channel, format and language | Where will this appear? Format, length limits, visuals, link possible? In which language do the readers read it? Existing text to improve? | Channel, format and the reader's language known; existing text pasted if any. |
| 8 | Voice | How does the brand sound? Words they use and avoid? Examples they like? | Tone in three words, or a brand brief loaded. |

## Real customer words

Ask for reviews, emails, DMs, call notes, survey answers. Keep phrases verbatim in the brief. If none exist, ask the user how a happy customer would describe it to a friend, and label that `inferred`.

## Gate 1

Show the checklist and a short brief (`templates/copy-brief.md`). Ask: "Does this match?" Continue only after confirmation.

# Worked examples

Fictional businesses, to show what moving from weak to strong looks like. Never copy these; write from the user's own customers.

## 1 · LinkedIn post, B2B service (bookkeeping for craftspeople)

**Before (score 38):**
> We are proud to announce that our bookkeeping office now also offers services for the construction sector! With over 15 years of experience we deliver quality, reliability and a personal approach. Interested? Contact us!

Weak: hook (about us, not the reader), problem in their words, outcome focus, customer language, show don't tell, first-screen rule. Crowded claims: quality, reliability, personal.

**After (score 84):**
> Saturday morning, 7:40. A carpenter in Rotterdam is sorting receipts on the kitchen table instead of being on site.
>
> We heard this story eleven times last year. So we built one thing: you photograph the receipt, we do the rest, and your VAT return is filed before you even think of it.
>
> Clients in construction now spend on average two hours less per week on admin. (Measured over 23 clients, 2025.)
>
> Know a craftsperson who does their books at the kitchen table? Send them this.

Why it works: a scene the reader recognises, their words ("receipts", "kitchen table"), one mechanism, a measured result with context, a call to action that fits the platform (share, not "contact us").

## 2 · Email subject lines, online course

**Before:** "Newsletter November" · "New course available!"
**After (variants to test):** "The 3 sourdough mistakes I made for a year" · "Why your bread stays flat (it's not the flour)" · "Your starter is fine. This is the problem."
Why: specific, about the reader's problem, curiosity that the email actually resolves.

## 3 · Landing page hero, heat pump installer

**Before:**
> Heat pumps from all A-brands · Professional installation · Request a quote

**After:**
> **Lower gas bill, without the subsidy headache.**
> We check your home first, tell you honestly whether a heat pump pays off, and handle the subsidy paperwork for you.
> [Book a free home check] · 4.8 from 112 reviews · Installed in 3 weeks on average

Why: promise in the reader's terms, mechanism (check first, honest advice), the real objection (subsidy paperwork) handled above the fold, proof next to the call to action.

## 4 · Instagram carousel outline, yoga studio

1. Hook: "Your back hurts after work. These 4 stretches take 6 minutes."
2 to 5. One stretch per slide, one sentence each, a clear photo.
6. "Why it works": one sentence of mechanism.
7. Soft call to action: "Save this for tonight. Want to do them with us? First class free, link in bio."
Why: each slide works on its own, value before the ask, a save-worthy format.

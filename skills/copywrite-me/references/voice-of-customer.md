# Voice of customer

The best copy is often already written by customers. This is how to find and sort it.

## Where to look
- Reviews of the user's business and of competitors (Google, marketplaces, app stores, Trustpilot)
- Emails, chat messages, DMs, sales call notes, support tickets
- Forum and community threads where the reader asks for help
- Survey answers ("What almost stopped you from buying?")
- The user's own customer conversations (Market This has a conversation script)

Public sources can be collected through a web search or Monid (quote and cap first, see `tools.md`). Private messages only from the user, anonymized.

## How to sort

Copy phrases verbatim into five buckets:

| Bucket | Listen for | Used in |
|--------|------------|---------|
| Pain | "I was so tired of...", "every time we..." | Lead, problem section, hooks |
| Desire | "I just wanted...", "finally..." | Promise, dream, headline |
| Objection | "I was afraid that...", "I almost didn't because..." | Objections, FAQ, final reassurance |
| Outcome | "Now I...", "since then..." | Proof, outcome focus, case |
| Words | The nouns and verbs they use for the problem and the product | Everywhere: replace the user's jargon |

## Rules
- Keep the original wording, including informal language, unless it is offensive or identifies a person.
- Note the source and date for each phrase.
- Use a full quote as a testimonial only with permission and the source's real name or role. Without permission, use the language, not the quote.
- Count: a phrase that appears in several sources is a stronger candidate for a headline.

# Self-check (visible to the user)

Run this before every gate summary and every delivery, and show the result to the user as a short block titled "Self-check". If an item fails, fix it first and say what you fixed. Never claim a check passed that you did not do.

## Checklist
1. **Claim register:** headline and hook promises count as claims too (outcomes, timeframes such as "in two hours", "finally"); each needs a register row or a `[to confirm]`. A hook that goes beyond what the user said is offered as a variant, not put in the final text unasked. every claim in the text (numbers, results, promises, process steps, guarantees, quotes) is in the claim register of the brief with its source. Anything the user did not supply is marked `[to confirm]` in the text.
2. **Quotes verbatim:** customer words are exactly as given; "something like" phrases are not presented as quotes.
3. **Channel check:** guidelines have source, date and confidence; Channel fit ratings follow the strict rules in `scorecard.md`.
4. **Honest score:** the yardstick matches the real format and length; nothing is n/a just to raise the score; you re-scored after the last change.
5. **One question:** your message ends with exactly one question (or the quick-request gap list).
6. **Complete text:** the final text is printed in full.
7. **Sector rules:** regulated claims checked against `sector-rules.md`.

## Claim register (in every delivery)
Show a short table: claim in the text, where it comes from (user said, document, public source), status (confirmed / [to confirm]).
